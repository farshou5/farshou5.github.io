your three notes

What changes, and why

1 · "Why 5 or 8 seconds? Make them 30."

Thursday night the couch scene was sent at 30 s twice, 15 s twice, 10 s once — all five came back failed (not a content block). Only 5 s and 8 s rendered. That is why the clips are fragments. It was never a choice.

v2: every scene segment is one take of up to 30 s, written as timestamped beats with ONE speaker per beat and a locked camera, and chained with Sequel inside a scene. First proof = the 30 s couch test queued today (0 credits, Unlimited).

2 · "Glasses appear and disappear. Lenses change. Food changes. Father's voice changes."

Every v1 clip was generated from text alone — no face, prop or voice reference attached (reference_elements: [] on all 49 jobs). Text cannot hold a face, a case or a voice steady across clips.

v2: a locked reference kit generated first, inside the project, and attached to every clip with Seedance roles (@Image 1 defines FATHER's face, hair, beard, clothing. Do not use the background.). One father, one son, one Layla, one child, one lens case, one table of food, one room, one future room. One voice sample per speaker attached as @Audio. No glasses at all (they were never in your film).

3 · "Go frame by frame. You are missing the points."

Done below — 1:20–3:28 at 2 fps, the rest at 1 fps, 38 contact sheets, every cut read for its why. The activation is: through the eye → into the brain → the effect → back on his face → his POV. The table is scanned item by item with the harm each one causes. The father is scanned for fatigue and stress, never a heart attack. The lenses are one small round silver case, and that same case is what he leaves on the grave.

per-clip checks

Nothing reaches your phone without passing these
  • Words: faster-whisper transcript of the clip vs the script line — every line must match.
  • Pace: words per second of each line; the father must sit around 2 w/s (slow). Faster → retake.
  • Face: contact sheet of the clip vs the reference card — same beard, same hair, no glasses, same clothes.
  • Props: the table, the case and the lens UI compared with the kit.
  • Logic: the take does what this page says the original does at that timecode.
  • Push: ADB to DCIM/Camera as NEURALSTAIN_U##_take#.mp4; a text line on Telegram, no video attachments.
